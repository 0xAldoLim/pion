# Try the new Pion learning flows

## Start with one idea

Open **Learn → Guided path → Start learning**. Read the current objective above the board, step through the demonstration, then try the idea yourself. The turn prompt names the side to move. You can click pieces or use **Keyboard move** below the board.

A wrong answer keeps your place and explains a concrete problem or the exercise objective. **Get a hint** reveals progressively more help. Reloading returns to the saved step with earlier hints and errors retained. Viewing, guided practice, independent transfer and later recall are separate observations. Every stage is available regardless of rating.

Use **Lesson library** to search directly. The coach's **Ask about this position** disclosure offers local explanations and candidate previews where appropriate; independent challenges keep assistance explicit and counted.

## Understand an opening

Open **Openings → Discover**, search **Italian**, and choose **Learn the ideas**. Follow the board, change the variation, and inspect plans for both sides. Start with **Short starting ideas** before extending the line.

Choose your practice color and add the line to your repertoire. **Guided ideas** explains the moves; recall hides the initial explanation; **Practice in a bot game** starts an unrated game from the chosen position. The opponent replies automatically between your turns. A Black-side learner waits for White's first move.

A different legal move can be a known alternative or receive a local-engine comparison. Choose **Retry intended move** to rehearse the selected line, or **Continue with this move** to explore. Exploration does not claim recall of a line you did not reproduce.

## Explore and remember

**Opening explorer** accepts legal board moves and keeps sibling branches when you go back and try another continuation. Transpositions connect identical positions reached through different move orders. Source badges distinguish installed book data, personal repertoire and local Stockfish candidates. Population statistics are not available.

**My repertoire** stores either color, folders, favorites, notes, custom PGN and due reviews. Reopening or saving a duplicate line preserves its existing schedule. After reviewing a game, save an instructive opening decision as a new repertoire branch.

## Apply the lesson

**Learn → Master studies** offers fourteen modern games with a pause, prediction, historical choice, comparison and replay. A different legal prediction is an idea to investigate. **Puzzles → Learn from the masters** retains the two verified Morphy mating exercises.

Finish a game and choose **Review this game → Run review**. Home's learning focus can then connect actual reviewed losses to lessons, short challenges and related puzzle themes. Small samples are observations rather than proof of general mastery.

## Choose your assistance

Settings independently controls practice evaluation, provisional classifications, coach reactions, hints and undo. Explicit practice hints remain visible even with automatic coach reactions off. Short-search classifications can change in a deeper review.

**Play now** opens the duration picker directly. The selected time affects either your local Blitz or Rapid standing. Active rated games hide evaluation, hints, coaching and engine variations; full review becomes available after the result is saved. Selectable practice bots leave both ratings unchanged. Opponent ratings remain strength estimates.

After **Offline ready** appears, essential lessons, repertoire, studies, puzzles and the lite engine work without a connection. Backups remain available in Settings; the full Stockfish pack is an optional installation.
