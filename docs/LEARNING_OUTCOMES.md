# Learning outcomes and evidence

22 October 2026. These measurements describe observed work. They do not measure comprehensive mastery or predict a human rating.

- **Recognition:** explanation choices, guided work and color-reflected relationships. New reflection successes cannot generate conceptual-application credit.
- **Application:** a first encounter with an authored different position, completed without hints or earlier errors. Count distinct positions, including across linked lessons, rather than repeated completion events.
- **Recall:** retrieving an already introduced idea. Spaced success additionally needs a prior successful lesson event, its due date and at least 24 hours since the previous success. Immediate repeats remain practice.
- **Real-game issues:** complete current unassisted reviews only. Loss markers require actual local search evidence. Duplicate imports with the same legal start/move history and perspective are one analytical example; changing names or IDs does not manufacture recurrence. Reimports cannot move an old observation into a newer trend window. Identical independently replayed games can therefore be conservatively undercounted. Saved records are never deleted.

Progress shows recognition, independent application, assisted practice, delayed recall and fresh puzzles separately. Application observations compare completed new positions, first-attempt errors and hints in non-overlapping periods. Unfinished attempts have no estimated outcome. Review trends use issues per own reviewed move and distinct histories, with sample sizes. Different opposition and conditions still limit comparisons.

Recommendations state the observed weakness, number of games/moves and source links, and offer its lesson, new challenge and related puzzles. One game is labeled a starting observation; recurrence requires distinct histories. Challenge recommendations prefer unseen authored tasks at an appropriate level, increasing fork complexity after unaided successes. All content remains freely accessible.

Existing progress and historical evidence are retained. Legacy reflection evidence is interpreted conservatively in skill profiles; no data is rewritten into a higher mastery state. Optional activity and sequence fields roundtrip through backup v4; versions 1–4 remain supported.

## Verification

Regression fixtures cover duplicate PGNs, genuinely different mating games, independent first attempts, assisted successes, delayed vs early recall, non-overlapping trends, repeated-position deduplication and preserved partial conversion backups. Browser journeys import/review duplicates, observe a single-game recommendation, and separately use genuinely distinct games to establish recurrence. These are correctness checks, not longitudinal proof of improved chess performance.
