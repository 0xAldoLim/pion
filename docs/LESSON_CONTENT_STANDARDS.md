# Lesson content standards

The initial curriculum has 31 original lessons across six unrestricted stages. Six foundation lessons cover coordinates, board vision, legal checks, attacks/defenses, values and en passant; the other five stages contain five lessons each. Earlier stable lesson identifiers remain available.

Each lesson has an objective, a short explanation, legal annotated demonstration, guided board check, explanation question, separate transfer position, practical summary and optional later recall. Correctness is the stated instructional objective, not a claim that a curated move is the unique engine best move. Curated tasks explicitly name their plan and acceptable alternatives. Rule-based tasks accept every legal move meeting their predicate.

Positions are validated with chess.js. Demo moves and intended answers are replayed in tests. Mate captions are checked against resulting legal-move/check state. The queen offer requires exactly one legal reply followed by checkmate. No diagram is attributed to a historical game unless it has a separately verified source.

Some transfer and recall diagrams reflect colors and ranks. This preserves legal pawn direction and teaches the opposite side, but is a closely related geometry; it is limited evidence, not broad mastery. Other introductory lessons use separately authored target arrangements. Independent evidence requires a fresh transfer position, zero hints and zero earlier errors. Later successful repeats measure recall separately.

All text is original and covered by the repository’s GPL-3.0-or-later license. Do not add copied course text, unsourced evaluations or GM quotations. Future content should prefer genuinely different structures and include local-engine verification when the objective depends on comparative move quality.
